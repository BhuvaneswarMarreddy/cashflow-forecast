# CashFlow Forecast - Logo Design Concepts

## Design Brief
**App Name:** CashFlow Forecast  
**Theme:** Deep Blue Professional  
**Purpose:** Personal finance management - tracking US loans, credit cards, income, investments, FDs, gold, silver  
**Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, focused on clarity and financial control

---

## Concept 1: **Flow Arrow with Chart**
**Description:**
- A stylized upward arrow (representing cash flow) integrated with a small chart/graph line
- The arrow flows from bottom-left to top-right (positive growth)
- Deep blue gradient (#1e40af → #3b82f6)
- Minimalist, modern
- Works well as icon and full logo

**Elements:**
- Arrow shape with subtle chart line inside
- Clean, geometric
- Single color or gradient

**Use Cases:**
- Favicon: Arrow icon only
- Logo: "CashFlow" text + arrow icon
- App icon: Arrow with subtle background

---

- Clean, corporate look

**Use Cases:**
- Favicon: Circular icon

## Recommended: **Concept 1 (Flow Arrow with Chart)**
**Why:**
- ✅ Clearly represents cash flow and forecasting
- ✅ Works great at small sizes (favicon)
- ✅ Professional yet approachable
- ✅ Matches the deep blue theme
- ✅ Scalable and recognizable
- ✅ Perfect for financial app

---

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** #1e40af (Deep Blue-800)
- **Secondary:** #3b82f6 (Blue-500)
- **Accent:** #60a5fa (Light Blue-400)
- **Background:** Transparent or subtle gradient
